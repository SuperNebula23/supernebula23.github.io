How To Play

Move Player

Use WASD, Arrow keys, or on-screen buttons to step around the grid.

Push Blocks

Walk into a numbered box to push it into empty space or onto a target tile.

Slide & Merge Matching Numbers

Pushing a box into another box with the SAME number merges them! (e.g. 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8).

Win Condition

Cover every target spot with a box matching or exceeding its required target value!
