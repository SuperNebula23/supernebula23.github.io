How to Play

1. Receive Loot: Adventurers enter your shop with various weapons, shields, and potions.

2. Rotate & Pack: Click/drag items into your backpack grid. Press R or right-click to rotate items to fit tight spaces.

3. Activate Synergies: Certain items boost adjacent items! For example, Gems double neighboring item values, and Helmets boost Weapons underneath them.

4. Pay Rent & Upgrade: Earn enough Gold each day to satisfy daily rent targets and expand your backpack grid!
